◆ Athletic Textile Innovation Lab • Manhattan

Biomechanical Fiber Innovation

Founded on the foundational premise that athletic socks are critical biomechanical equipment, SportFiberDawn designs engineered running hosiery that eliminates friction, absorbs ground impact, and regulates microclimate temperature.

Manhattan Laboratory

The Mercer Street Gait Testing Lab

Situated at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, our facility houses dual-belt instrumented force-plate treadmills, high-speed infrared thermal imaging cameras, and Martindale textile abrasion testers.

We reject mass-market tube socks stitched from coarse cotton threads. Every prototype developed in our studio undergoes multi-stage clinical trials measuring skin temperature, sweat evaporation rates, and footpad pressure distribution under grueling twenty-mile test runs.

Manufacturing Precision

200-Needle Italian Circular Knits

While ordinary department-store athletic socks use 96 to 144 needles, our production relies on high-gauge Lonati 200-needle circular knitting machines imported from Brescia, Italy.

This dense stitch count yields an ultra-fine, form-fitting sock architecture that conforms to individual foot anatomy like a second skin. By eliminating loose fabric slack, our socks prevent foot slippage inside shoes, eliminating the primary mechanical cause of blister shear.

Guild Standards

Our Four Performance Commitments

1. Zero Cotton

Cotton traps water against the skin; we use exclusively moisture-wicking merino wool and hydrophobic synthetics.

2. Seamless Rosso Toe

Zero raised seams across the toe cage, completely eliminating toe rub and digital blister points on downhill trails.

3. Degressive Compression

Calibrated 15-20 mmHg compression accelerates venous return and reduces calf muscle vibration fatigue.

4. 1,000-Mile Guarantee

Reinforced with Cordura nylon in high-wear heel and toe zones to ensure unmatched multi-season durability.
